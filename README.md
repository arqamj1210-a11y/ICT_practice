## What ICT Means to Me
For me, ICT is the bridge between people and technology. It is how information is created, shared, and used to solve real problems, from a simple message on a phone to systems that run businesses and hospitals.

As an ICT student, I see it as a field that combines learning, creativity, and problem-solving. It teaches me to think logically, work with tools like networks, software, and data, and turn ideas into something useful.

## My Goals

- Use ICT to make a positive impact in my community
- Grow into a professional who can guide and mentor other students

## Final Thought
ICT is not just a subject I study. It is the skill set I am using to shape my future career.
